# IT Modernization Services - Cloud Migration Support

**Agency:** Shared Services Canada
**Closes:** 2026-05-30 (42 days away)
**Estimated value:** $1,850,000
**Matched on:** cloud, AWS, IT modernization, DevOps

## Description

Shared Services Canada is seeking qualified contractors to support the migration of legacy financial systems to AWS GovCloud infrastructure. Scope includes discovery and assessment of approximately 40 legacy applications, migration planning, execution of lift-and-shift and re-platforming workloads, and knowledge transfer to internal SSC teams.

Mandatory requirements include demonstrated AWS migration experience with at least three prior federal or provincial engagements, AWS Advanced Partner status or equivalent, and bilingual delivery capability for technical documentation. Secret clearance required at the resource level for a subset of the work (est. 30% of hours). Contract term is 18 months with two one-year extension options at the Crown's discretion.
